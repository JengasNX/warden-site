WARDEN/THE PLATFORM

THE PLATFORM

Context for informed decisions.

WARDEN is being built as a connected digital trust platform, bringing reputation, guarantors, deals and report review into a shared history.

01

Reputation should be explainable

An allegation, an investigation and a confirmed violation are different states. Change history and an appeal process will help people understand the reasons behind decisions.

02

Deals and shared terms

Only verified users will be able to create deals. Both parties confirm one version of the terms and choose a guarantor. WARDEN does not hold their funds.

03

People retain control

Staff prepare cases for review. Owners make final dispute decisions and manage public reputation statuses. Automated systems do not make accusations.
