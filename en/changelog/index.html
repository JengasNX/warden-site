WARDEN, one step at a time.

Verified changes to the local project. Source publication and production deployment are separate events.

  1. 31 October 2026 · System pages and individual layouts

    Every public page received its own designed layout: the Premium pricing matrix, the status roadmap, the changelog timeline, an honest statistics panel, compare and review concepts, a guarantor directory preview and team positions. Added state pages: 404 (RU/EN), 403 access-denied, 503 maintenance, 423 suspended, 410 deactivated and the 500 error boundaries. Prices and statistics remain unfabricated.

  3. 30 October 2026 · Project foundation

    Created the monorepo, RU/EN shell, liveness endpoint, shared packages and CI. Build, types, lint, tests, HTTP routes and worker startup were verified. Business integrations and the database are not connected.
