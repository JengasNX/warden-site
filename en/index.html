04 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before you begin.

Visit the help center↗
01Can I use the platform yet?+

Not yet. The public interface is being developed. Registration, reputation checks, deals and evidence submissions will open after their modules have been implemented and verified.

02Does WARDEN hold participants’ funds?+

No. Initially WARDEN will help coordinate deals and record terms without holding participants’ funds. Any future escrow service requires a separate implementation and review.

03Does Premium increase reputation?+

No. Premium does not influence reputation, trust, reviews, moderation, violation classification or evidence decisions.

04Who makes violation decisions?+

Owners make final dispute decisions and control the three public reputation statuses. AI may provide recommendations but cannot make final decisions or assign statuses independently.
