Privacy as a design, not a promise.

The platform is designed around data minimization. A full privacy policy will follow legal review.

01

Data minimization

Only what is necessary for accounts, security and understandable feature behavior is collected. Data is never shared with third parties without an explicit basis and, where required, consent.

02

Nothing is being collected yet

Registration and business features are not active. Even when they launch, the composition of required data will be documented before implementation, not after.

03

Data rights

Data export, account deletion, consent history and versioned policies with clear retention are planned before public functionality opens.
